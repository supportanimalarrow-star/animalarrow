1 Introduction

Welcome to Animal Arrow, a puzzle game developed and published by Sazzidul Corporation.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use Animal Arrow, how advertising works, and how your information is handled.

Our current version is designed to work without user accounts or a developer-operated online database.

2 Information We Collect

In the current version of Animal Arrow:

Zero personal data collection on developer servers
  • We do not require account registration.
  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, home address or personal profile information.
  • We do not operate our own user-data collection server.
  • We do not maintain a personal user database.
  • We do not intentionally collect personal information directly through the game.

3 Data Stored on Your Device

Some game information may be saved locally on your device to provide a better gaming experience.

This may include:

  • Game progress and completed levels.
  • Coins, rewards and scores.
  • Game settings and preferences.
  • Locally saved gameplay information.

This information is stored on your device and is not uploaded to a Sazzidul Corporation server in the current version.

Important Note: Uninstalling the application or clearing its data may permanently delete locally saved progress.

4 Advertising and Third-Party Services

Animal Arrow may display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may process certain information to provide, measure and improve advertisements.

Depending on applicable settings, consent and regional requirements, this may include:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Device and technical information.
  • IP address.
  • Advertising interactions and impressions.
  • App diagnostics and performance information.

This information may be processed by Google and its partners under their own privacy policies.

Sazzidul Corporation does not operate the advertising infrastructure or independently control all information processed by third-party advertising providers.

5 Advertisements and Ad-Free Subscription

Animal Arrow may provide free gameplay supported by advertisements.

Users may purchase an eligible subscription through Google Play to remove advertisements.

When an active and valid ad-removal subscription is detected, eligible advertisements will be disabled according to the subscription benefits.

Please note:

  • Subscription payments are processed through Google Play.
  • Sazzidul Corporation does not directly receive or store your complete payment-card information.
  • Google Play manages payment processing and subscription status.
  • Subscription availability, pricing and billing periods will be displayed before purchase.

Third-party services may continue processing information according to their own policies even when advertisements are removed.
